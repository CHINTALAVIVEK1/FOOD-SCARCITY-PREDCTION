About the System

This system predicts food scarcity or surplus in different regions of India based on various factors:

  • Regional Selection: Choose from 36 Indian states and union territories
  • Crop Selection: Select from 37 major crops grown in India
  • Population Data: Regional population affects consumption needs
  • Environmental Factors: Rainfall and temperature affect crop yields
  • Production Capacity: Different regions have different production capacities for various crops
  • Consumption Patterns: Per capita consumption varies by crop type

The system uses a dataset with over 50,000 data points to train the prediction model, ensuring accurate and reliable results.
